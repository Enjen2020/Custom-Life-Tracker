Help

How to use each feature at the table.
Starting a game

Tap Start Game. Choose the number of players, tap 20, 30 or 40 for starting life (or type any number), name each seat or pick a saved profile, and choose the table layout so each card faces its player. Then tap Start Game. Quick Start reuses your last setup, and Resume Game continues a game you left.

Changing life

Tap the left or right side of a life total for −1 or +1. Hold either side to change it by 10 at a time. Quick taps are grouped, and the running change shows beside the total. Tap the middle to type an exact total. Several players can tap their own cards at the same time.

The center button

The round clock button where the cards meet opens the table menu: game time, Start / Pause / Resume timers, Game Log, Table Layout, turn direction, End Game, Help and Main Menu. A green dot on it means the timers are running; amber means paused. Undo, right beside it, reverses the latest action anywhere on the table (dice rolls aren't undone).

Turns and timers

Open the center button and tap Start Timer, then choose who goes first and the turn direction. The active player's card gets a highlighted border, their turn time and a Pass Turn button, which moves to the next player and skips anyone eliminated. Pause stops both clocks. If the app is closed mid-game, it reopens paused.

Your player menu

Tap on your card for: Customize (name, color, picture, card design, partner commander, and saved profiles to reuse), Counters, Commander Damage, Commander Tax, Monarch, Dungeon, Dice Roller (dice and coins), Look Up Card, Set Life and Eliminate. On small cards the menu opens over the table, still facing you.

Commander damage and counters

Fastest way: press and hold the middle of your life total, pick whose commander hit you, then adjust the amount; it changes your life too. Each source gets its own badge on your card; tap a badge to adjust it. Turn on Partner commander in Customize to track both partners. Commander Tax goes up 2 mana per step. Poison, experience, energy and rad live under Counters, where you can also add, rename or delete your own.

Dungeons and the initiative

Tap Dungeon in your player menu. To venture into the dungeon, pick Lost Mine of Phandelver, Dungeon of the Mad Mage or Tomb of Annihilation, then tap the next room each time you venture. Where the path splits, you choose. The map shows where you are and each room's effect. Reaching the last room completes the dungeon and adds it to your completed count. Take the initiative to venture into Undercity (or into the next room of the dungeon you're in). The player with the initiative gets a badge, and at the start of their upkeep they tap Venture into Undercity. If they're eliminated, the next player in turn order takes it. Undo reverses any of these.

Card lookup

Tap Look Up Card in your player menu (or Card Lookup on the main menu). Point the camera at a card, or search by name, to see its Oracle text, rulings, Commander details, printings and prices. It works offline once the card data is saved. From your player menu you can also set the card as your commander: its art becomes your picture and its name shows on your card. Close it to return to the game exactly as it was.

Warnings

A life total turns amber with “Low life” at a quarter of the starting life or less, and shows “Below zero” when negative. Poison at 7 or more and commander damage at 15 or more from one commander get a “!” badge saying how far they are from lethal.

Elimination

A player is eliminated automatically at 10 poison or 21 damage from a single commander. Life at 0 or below never eliminates automatically, because some cards keep players alive, so use Eliminate in the player menu when someone is out. Revive brings them back. Games never end on their own.

Seats and layout

Drag a card by its handle (the six dots) onto another seat to swap players; the card turns to face its new edge. Change the whole table layout any time from the center button's Table Layout.

Game log and history

Every life change, counter, commander damage, dice roll, elimination and card lookup is recorded in the Game Log (center button). To finish, open the center button, tap End Game and choose the winner or Draw. Your last 15 games are kept in Game History on the main menu.

Settings and offline use

Switch light or dark mode from the main menu. Settings has the accent color and Keep screen awake (on by default). Add the app to your home screen to use it like an app; it works without internet, including card lookup once the card data has been saved.
